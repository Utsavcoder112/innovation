Our Commitment to Sustainable Food Distribution

At Annadan, our mission is to revolutionize food distribution by creating a user-friendly platform that connects food suppliers with receivers. We are dedicated to ensuring that surplus food is efficiently utilized, significantly reducing food waste. Our core values revolve around sustainability, community empowerment, ethical practices, and innovation, as we leverage advanced technologies like the ChatGPT API to predict expiry dates accurately. By fostering a culture of sharing and mutual support, we aim to create a positive impact on food distribution and contribute to a more sustainable future.

About Our Project

Group Name: Sankalpa

We are Team Sankalpa, a group of passionate and purpose-driven students developing Annadan, an AI-based food donation web platform.

What is our project?

Our project, Annadan, is a technology-driven platform designed to reduce food wastage and address hunger by connecting food donors (like restaurants, hotels, households) with nearby NGOs, food banks, and people in need.

What problem does project solve?

Annadan addresses two major issues:

  • Food wastage in urban areas, especially from events, hotels, and restaurants.
  • Hunger and food insecurity among low-income individuals and homeless communities.

Introduction of the Project

Annadan is a smart, user-friendly web platform powered by AI that facilitates the donation and distribution of surplus food. It enables food donors to register and list excess food, while NGOs and receivers can track and access food, while both can stay updated about meal distribution events in real-time.

What problems are trying to solve, and why?

Globally and locally, enormous quantities of edible food are discarded daily while millions go hungry. Nepal, too, faces this imbalance. Our project aims to bridge this gap by offering a sustainable and scalable digital solution — to ensure that no food goes to waste while someone is going hungry.

Market Impact & Uniqueness

Global Market

Food redistribution platforms are growing worldwide, with apps like OLIO and Too Good To Go already making significant impacts. However, these are mostly commercial or localized.

Nepali Market

Nepal still lacks an AI-based structured platform dedicated to food donation. NGOs operate manually or through word of mouth. Annadan fills this digital gap.

What Makes Our Product Unique:

  • AI-powered chatbot assistance
  • Real-time food tracking and status check
  • Transparent donor-recipient matching
  • Local focus with future global scalability

Development Process

Ideation & Research

Identifying real-world issues through field interviews, surveys, and observation.

Design & Planning

Wireframing on Figma, feedback loops from mentors, and final UI/UX design.

Frontend Development

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for interactive and responsive design.

Backend Development

FastAPI for secure, fast RESTful services; integrated with MySQL to manage donor, food, and NGO records.

AI Integration

Developed a chatbot that supports users 24/7 by answering FAQs and guiding through donation steps.

Testing & Feedback

Carried out multiple iterations and made enhancements based on user experience testing.

Technologies Used

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Met by Our Project

Our project Annadan, developed by Team Sankalpa, contributes meaningfully to several United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). These are:

1. Goal 2: Zero Hunger

How Annadan supports this goal:

By connecting surplus food from donors (restaurants, households, etc.) to the hungry, Annadan directly fights food insecurity. The platform ensures that edible food reaches people in need rather than being wasted.

2. Goal 3: Good Health and Well-being

How Annadan supports this goal:

By delivering safe and surplus food to those who are malnourished or lack access to healthy meals, it supports better health outcomes.

Minimizes the environmental and public health impacts of food disposal.

3. Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

How Annadan supports this goal:

Supports urban sustainability by reducing food-related waste and promoting resource sharing.

Strengthens community engagement between donors and NGOs, fostering collaboration and social responsibility.

4. Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

How Annadan supports this goal:

Promotes responsible food consumption by encouraging donation of excess food.

Reduces food wastage through awareness and easy digital channels for redistribution.

Encourages businesses and households to rethink their food disposal habits.
